This product includes a compact, JSON-converted subset of dungeon and enemy
route data from Mythic Dungeon Tools (MDT), licensed under GNU GPL version 2.

Upstream project: https://github.com/Nnoggie/MythicDungeonTools
Source revision: 2a2df3bb28471ec9900942f1856215f85fdefe31
Included data: dungeon indexes, names, enemy identifiers and forces, enemy
clone indexes, group identifiers, sublevels, and map coordinates.

The upstream GPL-2.0 license text is provided in MDT-GPL-2.0.txt.
